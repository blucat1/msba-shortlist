- **Catalog coverage is partial:** 29 verified employer boards on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby are supplemented by one cross-employer remote publisher, Jobicy. The new publisher integration is deployed, but its first production import failed before network access; no Jobicy data is present from that run. After recovery it can expand discovery beyond selected employers, but it does not import the entire job market, all on-site/hybrid openings or every graduate program. Counts across feeds include different experience levels and do not establish graduate eligibility. See [SOURCE_AUDIT.md](SOURCE_AUDIT.md) for observed results. International-only, part-time, internships, contracts, unrelated jobs and people-leadership roles remain excluded. Employer popularity is not a fit signal.
- **External market searches are unverified:** Google/Indeed/LinkedIn links open their own search pages with user-entered terms and location. They do not import results, provide a verified count or enforce this site's 0–1-year policy. Entry-level keywords are only search terms; providers may require sign-in or return experienced roles. Check the original requirements before manually tracking a role in My Search.
- **Jobicy is a remote publisher:** its free API exposes a rolling seven-day discovery window, with about a three-hour publication delay. It does not supply the original employer ATS link through this free integration. Visible Jobicy credit and canonical links remain attached to records, saved snapshots and validated backups. Both save paths use a stable SHA-256 key from the normalized actual company name, avoiding one shared publisher company group; this is not legal-entity verification. Publisher publication time is not invented as the employer's original posting date. An “Anywhere” result does not establish US eligibility.
- **Graduate discovery is deliberately strict:** live **Experience focus** defaults to **New grad & 0–1 years**, using explicit graduate/entry-level evidence or a supported 0–1-year required minimum. One year is labeled as a requirement and a stretch for a zero-year user. Missing years, Associate/APM, Junior and generic Early Career titles alone are not proof of a graduate role. Required 2–3 years belong in the opt-in **Early career · 0–3 years** view; senior/4+ roles require **Experienced · 4+ / senior**; missing or conditional degree/experience paths appear in **Experience unverified**. Senior/Staff/Principal/Lead title evidence overrides low or unknown numeric minima. These filters preserve all saved My Search records, tasks and history.
- **Missing source fields stay unknown:** Greenhouse often supplies no employment type, so otherwise relevant roles may be in **Needs details checked**. Remote alone does not establish US eligibility. The source's actual restrictions and employer application page control eligibility. There is no work-authorization inference or sophisticated remote-eligibility engine.
- **Extraction is conservative:** a maintained rule taxonomy identifies supported title/context patterns. Unusual titles, salary prose, education alternatives or requirement formatting can be missed. Descriptions are bounded at 32,000 plain-text characters, so the employer original remains authoritative. Required/preferred skills are separated only when source wording supports it. Skills not listed by a user are not treated as proof of inability. Numeric title years do not produce graduation windows; unsupported timing fields remain unknown.
- **Compensation has uncertainty:** hourly/monthly/annual values and currencies remain separate. A bare dollar sign does not assert USD; a textual range without a clear period stays unknown. Unknown currency now renders safely as an explicit unknown after a formatter failure was found and fixed in the first hosted build. Structured salary tiers remain separate. Unknown applicable geography and base-versus-total evidence cannot promise a qualifying offer. No automatic hourly annualization occurs.
- **Dates are not invented:** Greenhouse edit timestamps and Ashby last-publication timestamps are preserved distinctly. Unknown original posting dates display first discovery/last verification. Fixture dates are simulated relative to a reference date and are never live posting evidence.
- **Ranking is a deterministic aid:** it reflects supplied preferences and available evidence, including explicit experience stretches for users with zero years. Internal scores are not hiring probabilities. Incomplete evidence cannot establish eligibility or predict outcomes. Current UI/catalog retrieval has an explicit 5,000-record recommendation cap and reports truncation; it must be revisited if monitored relevant listings grow beyond that cap.
- **Feeds can fail or change:** employer-board closure needs two complete successful absences at least 12 hours apart, with schema checks and mass-drop quarantine protecting prior records. Jobicy uses separate publisher status checks for every aged-out nonclosed ID: window absence never closes a listing; explicit `closed` does; `unknown` becomes unconfirmed and is withheld from recommendations without advancing last seen. These statuses are not direct employer-ATS verification. Partial, failed or malformed batches preserve prior data. Responses over 24 MB or Lever boards exceeding 200 pages are rejected safely. The earlier [29-board recovery workflow](https://github.com/blucat1/msba-shortlist/actions/runs/39583462319) predates this rolling-feed release; [TEST_REPORT.md](TEST_REPORT.md) records separate acceptance.
- **Freshness depends on providers:** the pilot remains twice daily at 06:23/18:23 UTC, not continuous or hourly. Jobicy production ingestion skips if persisted `last_check_at`, including a recorded failure, is less than 60 minutes old or future/invalid; skips do not advance health. Exactly 60 minutes permits retry. Credential-free audits/dry-runs or unrecorded failed attempts still require coordination within the provider's at-most-hourly limit. GitHub schedules may delay/drop or disable after inactivity, and free Supabase projects can pause. A 36-hour source becomes stale; after 72 hours its jobs leave default recommendations. The isolated demo remains available, while live failures and zero results remain honest.
- **Usage rights need owner review:** public APIs and career-page links do not grant unrestricted republication. Jobicy's documented fair use requires visible credit, canonical links and its synchronization limit. Source attribution, bounded text, usage notes and disable controls are implemented; there is no claim of legal vetting or universal permission. Review current source terms and disable sources requiring permission the owner lacks.
- **Application submission is manual:** opening an application page does not mark Applied or contribute to application analytics. Users explicitly confirm submission. There is no application autofill, authenticated scraping, LinkedIn/Handshake scraping, employer login, Gmail integration, email sending or calendar invitation creation. Unsupported URLs are stored as links; a privileged server never fetches arbitrary user URLs.
- **Demo storage is local:** fictional fixtures and user edits live in namespaced localStorage for that browser/origin. Clearing browser storage, private browsing cleanup or storage quotas can lose demo changes; export JSON first. Demo records never merge into real accounts or enter the production catalog. A publicly reachable demo still uses browser-local sample data.
- **Pilot scale and spending remain bounded by providers:** compact summaries, on-demand descriptions, bounded imports and retention reduce usage. The approved setup remains Free, with no automatic upgrade added by this project. Owner-only merges, protected secrets and disabled previews do not cap public visitors or guarantee zero charges. Cloudflare account billing returned 403, so unrelated paid subscriptions were not verified. Review actual quotas and provider spending controls.
- **Write access remains a trust decision:** only `blucat1` currently has repository access; no teammate invitations were sent. Verified rules permit only the owner to merge `main` and keep mandatory CI separate from review bypass. Environment secrets allow only branch `main`, with no repository copies; manual production dispatch/reruns are owner-gated. Future writers can still run/cancel CI. Public code can be copied, and CODEOWNERS does not promise exclusive authorship or control all public usage. See [COLLABORATION.md](COLLABORATION.md).
- **Deletion and backups:** the protected function's authenticated deletion and private-data cascades passed the hosted disposable-account checks. Provider backup retention can outlast deletion. User JSON exports are private portable records, not an automatic full Supabase/Auth disaster-recovery backup.
