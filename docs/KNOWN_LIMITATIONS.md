# Known limitations

This is a complete local demo plus real integration code, not a statement that a public live service has been deployed. Follow [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md) and [TEST_REPORT.md](TEST_REPORT.md) before inviting users.

- **Hosted acceptance remains:** live Google OAuth consent/callbacks, hosted persistence, deployed account deletion, scheduled production imports and two-user hosted isolation have not been accepted. Docker was absent during the initial Mac build (Supabase CLI 2.121.0 was installed/version-verified), so that original local SQL attempt was not executed. The follow-up [GitHub Supabase authorization job](https://github.com/blucat1/msba-shortlist/actions/runs/39568999347/job/119118184024) did start a real disposable Supabase stack and passed **2 SQL suites / 93 pgTAP tests** on October 21, 2026 at **01:26:50 PDT**. The real local/test SQL gate is passed; the simulated database roles in that suite do not replace hosted checks with two independent Google accounts.
- **Coverage is deliberately narrow:** the initial registry has 26 researched, verified employer identities on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby. This is neither the US labor market nor all early-career MSBA roles. See the dated real counts in [SOURCE_AUDIT.md](SOURCE_AUDIT.md). International-only, part-time, internships, contracts, unrelated jobs and people-leadership roles are excluded. Senior IC jobs need the larger-stretches control. Employer popularity is not a fit signal.
- **Missing source fields stay unknown:** Greenhouse often supplies no employment type, so otherwise relevant roles may be in **Needs details checked**. Remote alone does not establish US eligibility. The source's actual restrictions and employer application page control eligibility. There is no work-authorization inference or sophisticated remote-eligibility engine.
- **Extraction is conservative:** a maintained rule taxonomy identifies supported title/context patterns. Unusual titles, salary prose, education alternatives or requirement formatting can be missed. Descriptions are bounded at 32,000 plain-text characters, so the employer original remains authoritative. Required/preferred skills are separated only when source wording supports it. Skills not listed by a user are not treated as proof of inability. Numeric title years do not produce graduation windows; unsupported timing fields remain unknown.
- **Compensation has uncertainty:** hourly/monthly/annual values and currencies remain separate. A bare dollar sign does not assert USD; a textual range without a clear period stays unknown. Structured salary tiers remain separate. Unknown applicable geography and base-versus-total evidence cannot promise a qualifying offer. No automatic hourly annualization occurs.
- **Dates are not invented:** Greenhouse edit timestamps and Ashby last-publication timestamps are preserved distinctly. Unknown original posting dates display first discovery/last verification. Fixture dates are simulated relative to a reference date and are never live posting evidence.
- **Ranking is a deterministic aid:** it reflects supplied preferences and available evidence, including explicit experience stretches for users with zero years. Internal scores are not hiring probabilities. Incomplete evidence cannot establish eligibility or predict outcomes. Current UI/catalog retrieval has an explicit 5,000-record recommendation cap and reports truncation; it must be revisited if monitored relevant listings grow beyond that cap.
- **Feeds can fail or change:** complete-board/schema checks, bounded retries and mass-drop quarantine favor preserving records over rapid closure. Closure needs two successful absences at least 12 hours apart. A source may therefore remain unconfirmed longer than its employer posting. A healthy endpoint does not prove employer identity; registry official-page evidence does. Responses over 24 MB or Lever boards exceeding 200 pages are rejected safely and require an operator to review limits.
- **Freshness depends on providers:** GitHub schedules may delay/drop or disable after inactivity, and free Supabase projects can pause. A 36-hour source becomes stale; after 72 hours its jobs leave default recommendations. The isolated demo remains available, while live failures and zero results remain honest.
- **Usage rights need owner review:** public APIs and career-page links do not grant unrestricted republication. Source attribution, bounded text, original links, usage notes and disable controls are implemented; there is no claim of legal vetting or universal permission. Review employer terms before public launch and disable sources requiring permission the owner lacks.
- **Application submission is manual:** opening an application page does not mark Applied or contribute to application analytics. Users explicitly confirm submission. There is no application autofill, authenticated scraping, LinkedIn/Handshake scraping, employer login, Gmail integration, email sending or calendar invitation creation. Unsupported URLs are stored as links; a privileged server never fetches arbitrary user URLs.
- **Demo storage is local:** fictional fixtures and user edits live in namespaced localStorage for that browser/origin. Clearing browser storage, private browsing cleanup or storage quotas can lose demo changes; export JSON first. Demo records never merge into real accounts or enter the production catalog. A publicly reachable demo still uses browser-local sample data.
- **Pilot scale is bounded:** full board records support honest absence/reclassification; descriptions load on demand, summaries are compact, imports are bounded, and retention prunes obsolete diagnostics/unreferenced details. Free tiers do not guarantee unlimited capacity or uptime. No automatic paid upgrade, monitoring subscription or billing plan is enabled.
- **Deletion and backups:** the protected function deletes the authenticated account and cascaded private data; real hosted behavior still needs a disposable-account check. Provider backup retention can outlast deletion. User JSON exports are private portable records, not an automatic full Supabase/Auth disaster-recovery backup.
- **Validation scope:** automated tests and any local screenshots are not fabricated user testing or accessibility certification. Real Google accounts, two-user cloud privacy, provider limits and deployed mobile behavior remain owner acceptance work. Record team feedback and any resulting changes in the demo script rather than inventing a participant or outcome.

The app is independent of Cal Poly and does not promise interview, offer or employment outcomes. The owner contact must be configured with a real monitored destination before public launch.
