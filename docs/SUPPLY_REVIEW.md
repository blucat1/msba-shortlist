# Supply and qualification review — 22 October 2026

The initial 29-employer registry was a pilot, not the entire market. The user has now authorized wider coverage. This change adds eight verified employer boards, prioritizing actual graduate supply and current employer requirements. There are now **37 enabled employer boards, one enabled cross-employer Jobicy source, and two disabled candidate boards**. This is still incomplete market coverage.

The target cohort graduates **15 May 2027** and can start **16 May 2027**. The default experience policy accepts explicit graduate/entry-level recruitment or a required lower bound of 0–1 years. A one-year or six-month requirement remains visible. The separate confirmed-zero view requires an explicit zero/no-experience requirement; a graduate title with unstated years never creates a numeric zero. Cohort, degree, GPA and authorization checks remain separate individual constraints.

## Current-employer contradiction

[Jobicy 153373](https://jobicy.com/jobs/153373-financial-analyst-entry-level) was captured with graduate/entry-level copy. The [current CertiK employer posting](https://jobs.lever.co/certik/7d25f5e8-2fed-412c-b182-6568a491483e) instead requires 1–2 relevant professional years in both its overview and qualifications.

`data/listing-reviews.json` records this conflict against the exact publisher source, external ID and title, with the current employer evidence URL and check time. Publisher text and publication date are preserved. The conflicting publisher row is withheld from default recommendations and left available for explicit review. A later title update does not silently inherit a review for different publisher copy. The direct posting's one-year floor must remain visible, and publication on an aggregator is not evidence of the employer's original posting date.

* [WisdomTree careers](https://www.wisdomtree.com/us/careers-at-wisdomtree) now verifies its exact Lever identity and three current roles. Its graduate-or-experienced AI Solutions Analyst is a useful known miss, but the [corporate terms](https://www.wisdomtree.com/us/terms) restrict reproduction of corporate-site information without consent and defer outbound sites to their own terms. Applicability to identical ATS content remains unresolved despite Lever's public third-party access documentation. The registry row is **candidate and disabled for usage clarification**, with identity evidence recorded; no request for consent was sent. A graduate OR experienced path is not an unconditional one-year minimum.
* Pathward, KBRA, BarkleyOKRP and Clearway supplied useful actual qualification examples for the offline benchmark. Their inclusion there is **not registry verification or authorization for production ingestion**. Additional corporate identity/usage review remains necessary before enabling them. ERG was subsequently verified and enabled as described above; the benchmark labels were not changed.
* Workday, iCIMS, Taleo, SuccessFactors, SmartRecruiters and custom employer systems remain major gaps. Large-bank, consulting, retail, healthcare and government hiring is therefore underrepresented. A source being public does not make its site an approved arbitrary scraping target.

Jobicy provides cross-employer discovery within its free remote feed, not the whole US market. Its seven-day publication window and publication delay are documented; absence from that window cannot close a listing. Old IDs need explicit status checks, with unknown status withheld rather than presumed active or closed. Anywhere listings do not establish US eligibility. The existing no-more-than-hourly synchronization rule applies.

No ongoing, no-account/no-key source permitting broad onsite/hybrid job-board reuse was verified as a suitable whole-market replacement in this review. Complete US-market coverage cannot be promised from the present sources. The supported path is more verified employer boards and separately reviewed public APIs, or an owner-approved licensed aggregator when its cost and usage terms fit the budget.

Public read API references: [Greenhouse Job Board API](https://developers.greenhouse.io/job-board.html), [Lever public postings API](https://github.com/lever/postings-api), [Ashby public job posting API](https://developers.ashbyhq.com/docs/public-job-posting-api). These support technical access; they do not grant an unlimited license to republish employer descriptions. Production discovery remains attributed summaries with original application links, complete validated snapshots, no application-submission API, and no accounts, fees or new credentials for these additions.

## Source-reviewed diagnostic benchmark

`data/quality-benchmark-v1.json` contains **101 actual posting reviews from 14 employers across 15 boards/feeds**. Labels came from assistant manual source reading, not the classifier's existing labels. It is **assistant-reviewed**, not independently human/student validated. Classmate testing was explicitly deferred.

The sample contains 24 included, 26 Needs details and 51 excluded cases. Experience labels are scored for 50 in-scope/uncertain cases; excluded internships, generic pipelines, unrelated specialist work and foreign openings are not meaningful scored experience-tier decisions. Ten sample rows qualify for the strict experience/scope default and one for confirmed numeric zero. This sample is deliberately rich in negative/conditional cases, not proportionate to production or the market.

Each row retains source identity, a real URL/ID, the expected scope/default decisions, independent review notes, restrictions and a separate cohort-timing review. `rawInput` contains provider metadata and a bounded verbatim requirements excerpt. It contains no synthesized requirements and no copied full description. Salary text is omitted because this benchmark does not evaluate compensation. Titles plus unique description excerpts are capped at 25 words per posting; the evidence object points to the retained excerpt instead of duplicating it.

Coverage includes six-month minima, word-number and parenthesized years, zero-to-five ranges, preferred-only years, multiple independent requirements, degree/experience alternatives, mixed Analyst/Senior Analyst paths, 2028 intern versus 2027 graduate cohorts, foreign graduate programs, clinical/software roles, missing employment/country evidence and current-employer contradictions. Actual source reading also exposed a shared-year May/June 2027 extraction bug that could wrongly exclude May 15 graduates; separate eligibility regressions cover its correction.

Observed local check: `npm run quality:check -- --output /tmp/msba-quality-supply-final.json` passed classifier 2026-10-22.1 on this captured sample: strict TP 10, FP 0, FN 0, TN 91; scope 101/101; applicable experience 50/50; confirmed-zero decisions 101/101. These are diagnostic regression results on selected bounded passages. **They do not estimate market-wide recall, full-posting extraction accuracy, live job availability, candidate eligibility, interview outcomes or actual cloud acceptance.** Parent release checks and the recorded production import determine deployed counts separately.
